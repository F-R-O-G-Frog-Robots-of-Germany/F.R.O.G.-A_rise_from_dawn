  {\Large\bfseries Flywheel reduction\par}
  \smallskip
  We assumed an average motor speed of \(3{,}000\)~RPM and an estimated
  average flywheel speed of \(2{,}500\)~RPM.
  \[
    \begin{aligned}
      \text{Reduction factor} & =
        \frac{\text{motor speed}}{\text{estimated average flywheel speed}} \\
      & = \frac{3{,}000}{2{,}500} = \boxed{1.2}
    \end{aligned}
  \]
  \textbf{\(1{:}1.2\) reduction} (flywheel turns to motor turns).\par
