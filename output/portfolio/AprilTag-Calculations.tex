{\large\bfseries AprilTag Pose from a Tilting Hive\par}
{\small \(T\) tag, \(C\) camera, \(R\) robot.
 \(T_{AB}\) maps points from \(B\) to \(A\).
 \(R_{AB}\) is its rotation. Distances are in inches.\par}
\medskip

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.33\linewidth}
\raggedright
\textbf{1. Locate the hinge}\par
\smallskip
\(\begin{aligned}[t]
T_{RT} &= T_{RC} T_{CT}\\
h_R &= T_{RT}(d_T)\\
u_R &= R_{RT}a_T
\end{aligned}\)\par
\smallskip
\small
\key{T_{CT},\ T_{RC}}{Limelight 3D tag pose and known camera mount.}
\key{d_T,\ a_T}{Hinge point and signed axis in tag coordinates.}
\key{h_R,\ u_R}{The same hinge point and axis in robot coordinates.}
\end{minipage}\hfill
%
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.28\linewidth}
\raggedright
\textbf{2. Find heading}\par
\smallskip
\(\displaystyle\psi=-\operatorname{atan2}(u_{R,y},u_{R,x})\)\par
\smallskip
\small
\key{\psi}{Robot heading in radians.}
\key{u_{R,x},\ u_{R,y}}{Forward and left components of the hinge axis.}
Robot roll and pitch are set to \(0^\circ\).
The signed hinge axis points along field \(+X\).
\end{minipage}\hfill
%
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.34\linewidth}
\raggedright
\textbf{3. Find field position}\par
\smallskip
{\setlength{\fboxsep}{3pt}%
\(\boxed{p_{xy}=H_{xy}-R_2(\psi)\,h_{R,xy}}\)}\par
\smallskip
\small
\key{p_{xy},\ H_{xy}}{Robot and fixed hinge positions on the field.}
\key{h_{R,xy}}{Robot-to-hinge offset in the robot's horizontal plane.}
\key{R_2(\psi)}{2D rotation by the robot heading.}
\end{minipage}\par

\medskip
{\small\textbf{Synthetic check at \(+30^\circ\) Hive tilt}\par
 \(h_{R,xy}=(47.32,-20)+(-17.32,0)=(30,-20)\), \quad
 \(u_{R,xy}=(0,-1)\Rightarrow\psi=90^\circ\), \quad
 \(p_{xy}=(60,0)-(20,30)=(40,-30)\).\par
 At \(-30^\circ\), \(0^\circ\) and \(+30^\circ\), the synthetic test recovers
 the same robot pose.\par}
\end{adjustbox}
\end{minipage}%
